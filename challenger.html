Electric bike for Indian roads

The BNC Challenger is a multipurpose electric bike on an exoskeletal steel chassis. Its battery lifts out, charges from any 6A socket and runs up to 180 km.

Models: S110 and S125

The S110 runs up to 90 km on a 2.1 kWh battery. The S125 carries 4.2 kWh for up to 180 km.

Etrol 40, the battery you lift out

  1. Lift out the Etrol 40

    The pack is removable. Carry it indoors by its handle.

  2. Charge from any 6A socket

    The portable charger plugs into a standard 6A socket, at home or at work.

  3. Covered for 5 years

    IP67 rated, tested above 300°C internal temperature and compliant with AIS-156 Amendment 3, Phase 2. The standard warranty runs 5 years.
