As the electric vehicle (EV) industry grows, the demand for efficient, durable, and cost-effective EV batteries is higher than ever.

Enter Etrol40—a removable, IP67-rated LFP battery designed to provide longer range, faster swapping, and hassle-free charging. Learn more about Etrol40.

Whether you're navigating urban streets or tackling rural terrains, Etrol40 ensures seamless performance and unmatched convenience.

Why Etrol40 is a game-changer for EV owners

Removable & swappable – instant power anytime

One of the biggest concerns for EV riders is charging downtime. With Etrol’s advanced battery-swapping technology, you can swap a depleted battery in under two minutes—ensuring zero waiting time and uninterrupted rides.

IP67-rated for extreme durability

Etrol40 is built to last. Waterproof and dustproof, this rugged EV battery withstands harsh weather conditions and tough terrains, making it an ideal choice for both city commuters and off-road riders.

LFP battery technology – safer, longer-lasting performance

Unlike conventional lithium-ion batteries, Lithium Ferrous Phosphate (LFP) technology offers:

  • Longer battery lifespan
  • Enhanced thermal stability (reducing overheating risks)
  • Improved safety and efficiency

Lower ownership costs – affordable & hassle-free

With Etrol’s cost-effective battery-swapping model, you save on energy costs while enjoying uninterrupted rides. No need for expensive charging stations or long waiting hours—just swap and go!

Say goodbye to range anxiety

Etrol’s growing battery-swapping network ensures you’re never far from a power source. Locate nearby swapping stations with the user-friendly Etrol app, so you’re always in control of your journey.

The future of electric mobility starts here

Etrol40 is revolutionizing EV adoption by eliminating charging worries, reducing range anxiety, and making electric mobility more accessible and cost-efficient. Whether you're a daily commuter or a long-distance traveler, Etrol40 gives you the freedom to ride smarter and farther.
