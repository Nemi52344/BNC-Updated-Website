Good questions

How far does it go on one charge?
Up to 90 km on one Etrol 40 pack, and up to 180 km on two. The same for the Challenger and the Messenger.
Where can I charge it?
At any standard 6A wall socket, with the portable charger. The battery lifts out by its handle, so you can charge it at home, at the shop or at work.
How safe is the battery?
Very. The Etrol is tested to more than 300°C continuous internal temperature and to extreme electrical abuse in worst-case failure modes. It is IP67 rated and meets AIS-156 Amendment 3, Phase 2.
How much can it carry?
A lot. The Challenger carries 200 kg and more on its steel frame. The Messenger C110 is rated for 250 kg.
What does it cost?
The Challenger starts at ₹99,900 and the Messenger C110 at ₹89,999, both ex-showroom. Pricing is subject to change prior to delivery.
What is the warranty?
7 years on the chassis, 5 on the battery and 3 on the motor.
Where can I buy one or get it serviced?
At BNC dealers in 24 cities. Tell us your city and we connect you with the nearest one.
