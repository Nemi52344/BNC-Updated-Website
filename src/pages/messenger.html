The Messenger C110

Built to carry.
Made to last.

The Messenger C110's unique capabilities open doors to destinations once thought unreachable. Great mileage, low maintenance and powerful performance, on a rugged bike that carries high loads.

Explore the Specs
01 Versatility redefined

A 900 mm seat, the longest in the industry

The Messenger C110 ensures comfort and adaptability for both commuting and off-road adventures, with room for a passenger and for cargo.

02 Built for heavy-duty performance

Carries up to 250 kg with ease

Its 14-degree gradeability and use of common parts with ICE two-wheelers ensure dependable, low-maintenance performance.

03 Flexible charging, anywhere

Charge on the go. No fixed infrastructure.

With a removable Etrol 40 battery and a portable charger, the Messenger C110 lets riders charge on the go, with no need for fixed charging infrastructure.

04 Engineered for any challenge

Exceptional reliability, even on rough roads

With a powerful motor, the Messenger C110 gives a smooth ride and comfortably handles tough terrain. It suits any rider.
